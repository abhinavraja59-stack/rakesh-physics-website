How to Start Your JEE Journey

A practical JEE preparation plan can begin with a clear academic routine.

1. Build the Fundamentals

Focus first on understanding the core concepts of Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics.

2. Practise Regularly

Solve questions consistently and maintain a record of mistakes and difficult topics.

3. Test Yourself

Chapter-wise and full-length tests help students measure progress and improve examination readiness.

4. Analyse Performance

Test marks are useful only when students identify why they made mistakes and work systematically on those areas.

5. Stay Consistent

Long-term consistency is one of the most important parts of any competitive examination preparation journey.
